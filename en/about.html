About Me

Every day I work alongside an AI agent (Claude Code), but I'm the one who reviews and takes responsibility for what ships to production. After almost 6 years in PHP, I know tools change all the time — responsibility for code quality always stays with the developer.

How I got here

I started out as an IT technician, then studied information systems engineering at the University of Warmia and Mazury in Olsztyn. I've been coding commercially in PHP since 2020. I began with smaller e-commerce and CRM projects, and today I work on international platforms and systems where sensitive data, high traffic and payment integrations matter.

How I work

I feel most at home in projects where you need to add new code to an existing system without breaking anything. In commercial work I prefer proven, stable solutions over whatever's trendy. What matters is that production keeps running.

Versatility

Beyond PHP, I've also tried Ruby on Rails (completed The Odin Project course), some Python/Flask, Go, and even mobile development — Kotlin, Swift, Flutter. I've also dabbled in AI/Data Science. It makes it easier for me to understand the whole system, not just the piece I happen to be responsible for.

I work with Claude Code every day — for refactoring, code review, testing. I treat it as another tool in the toolbox, not a passing trend.

How I work in practice

A good example of how I work: on one project, the checkout payment process on a Shopify store stopped working. Customers couldn't complete purchases, and the company was losing sales by the hour. The person previously responsible for that integration had simply disappeared — no handover, no documentation. The client was left alone with the problem. The code was in Python/Flask with the Shopify API — outside my main specialization.

I sat down with unfamiliar code, found the bug, and shipped the fix the same day. Payments were back before the losses became serious.

I didn't wait for someone "who knows Python" to handle it. And I'd rather not leave a client alone with a problem, the way they were left before.
