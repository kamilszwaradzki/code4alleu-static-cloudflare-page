Privacy policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

1. Data controller

The controller of your personal data is Kamil Szwaradzki, owner of code4all.eu. For any matter concerning your personal data, write to kontakt@code4all.eu.

2. What data I process and why

Contact form, email and phone. When you send a message through the contact form, email me or call me, I process the data you give me yourself: your name or company name, email address, phone number and the content of your message. I use it only to reply to your message and to continue our correspondence.

The legal basis is my legitimate interest in replying to messages sent to me (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR) and, if you are asking about working together, taking steps at your request before entering into a contract (Art. 6(1)(b) GDPR).

Providing your data is voluntary, but without an email address or phone number I will not be able to reply.

Server logs. The website is hosted on Cloudflare. Each visit automatically records basic technical information such as your IP address, browser type and time of the visit. This is used only to keep the website running and secure (Art. 6(1)(f) GDPR). I do not use it to identify visitors.

3. Who receives the data

I do not sell your data or share it with anyone for marketing purposes. Data may only be received by companies that technically operate the website and email:

  • Web3Forms — a service that forwards contact form messages to my email address,
  • Cloudflare, Inc. — website hosting,
  • the provider of my email inbox, where messages are delivered.

Some of these companies are based or have servers outside the European Economic Area (including the USA). In that case, data is transferred on the basis of the mechanisms set out in Chapter V GDPR, such as the European Commission's standard contractual clauses or the EU-US Data Privacy Framework adequacy decision.

4. How long I keep the data

I keep correspondence data for as long as the correspondence requires, and then for no longer than 12 months after the last message. If the correspondence concerns entering into or performing a contract, I may keep it until any related claims become time-barred.

5. Your rights

You have the right to:

  • access your data and receive a copy of it,
  • have your data rectified,
  • have your data erased,
  • restrict processing,
  • data portability,
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest.

To exercise these rights, write to kontakt@code4all.eu. You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a supervisory authority — in Poland, the President of the Personal Data Protection Office (ul. Stawki 2, 00-193 Warsaw, uodo.gov.pl), or the authority in your country of residence.

I do not make automated decisions about you or profile you.

6. Cookies

This website does not use cookies or analytics tools and does not store any information in your browser. Fonts, styles and scripts are loaded directly from the website's own server, without third-party providers.

7. Links to other services

The website contains links to my profiles on LinkedIn, GitHub and Facebook. When you follow such a link, you go to a service that processes data under its own privacy policy.

8. Changes to this policy

If the way the website processes data changes, I will update this policy. The current version is always available on this page.
